Getting Started with CaseNoter

Choose what you need below. Follow one step at a time.

Set up CaseNoter once

Already set up? Choose Write a note above.

1. Download CaseNoter

You need a Windows computer and an internet connection.

Download CaseNoter
For Housing and Education & Employment staff. Version 3.0.4.

2Open the download

Open your Downloads folder. Double-click CaseNoter-Setup.exe.

Follow the instructions on screen. Keep the suggested folder.

If Windows blocks it, or the suggested folder does not work, ask Dane for help.

3Choose your team

Choose Housing or Education & Employment (EE&T).

Finish Setup. Stay connected to the internet until it finishes.

4Open CaseNoter

Open CaseNoter when Setup offers to. You can also double-click the new CaseNoter icon on your desktop.

Next, open just the section below that applies to you.

I already use CaseNoter — click to open

1Open your old CaseNoter

Open it the way you usually do. Finish saving your notes.

Leave the old CaseNoter and its black window open.

2Start the move in the new CaseNoter

Click Move my existing setup.

The app finds your old setup. If it shows more than one, choose the one you usually use.

Example only. Click the picture to make it larger.

3Allow the move

A request opens in your web browser.

Check that the transfer code is the same in both windows. In your browser, click Allow this one-time transfer.

If it opens under a different browser account than you usually use, ask Dane for help.

Example only. Click the picture to make it larger.

4Enter your usual CaseNoter password

Go back to the new CaseNoter. Enter your existing CaseNoter password.

Click Import and open CaseNoter. Wait for the app to open.

If it asks to replace a setup, continue only if you mean to replace that setup. Ask Dane if you are unsure.

5Check before you finish

Your passwords stay the same. Check that your team and participants look right.

If you use both teams, check both before removing the old version.

Open Pending requests to review unfinished work. It is not sent to Apricot automatically. Check Apricot before trying those requests again.

If the move fails: keep your old version and ask Dane for help.

I am new to CaseNoter — click to open

You do this only the first time. You will use your own Apricot account.

1Choose a CaseNoter password

Click Set up a new account. Follow the instructions to choose a password and enter it again.

Keep this password private. You will need it each time you open CaseNoter.

Example only. Click the picture to make it larger.

2Open Bonterra

Bonterra provides the connection details CaseNoter needs to use Apricot.

Open the Bonterra link on the setup screen, or click here to open Bonterra.

Sign in with your own account. Dane can help, but you must sign in and create these details yourself.

Click Manage APIs, then Create credentials.

Example only. Click the picture to make it larger.

3Fill in the Bonterra form

  1. Choose Apricot as the product.
  2. Enter a name, such as casenote.
  3. Choose your organization.
  4. Click Create credentials.

Example only. Click the picture to make it larger.

4Copy the two details into CaseNoter

Bonterra shows two details: Client ID and Client Secret.

  1. Copy the Client ID. Paste it into the CaseNoter box with the same name.
  2. Copy the Client Secret. Paste it into the CaseNoter box with the same name.
Keep the Bonterra window open until you have copied both. Bonterra will not show the Client Secret again. If you lose it, you must create a new one.

Example only. Click the picture to make it larger.

5Finish and wait

Return to CaseNoter and follow the remaining instructions.

Loading your participants may take a few minutes. Wait for it to finish.

The new connection details may need time to start working. If CaseNoter asks you to wait or retry, follow that message. Ask Dane if you cannot continue.

Forgot your password? Ask Dane before resetting. Resetting removes unfinished work from this computer.

Write a note

These steps are for Housing staff. Education & Employment forms may look different; ask Dane if you need help.

1Open CaseNoter

Double-click the CaseNoter icon on your desktop, or open it from the Windows Start menu.

Enter your password. Click Unlock.

2Find the person

Type the person's name or HMIS number into the search box. Click their name.

Only people with a Housing enrollment appear.

3Choose Contact Documentation

Click Contact Documentation.

Meeting Narrative is being phased out and cannot be selected.

Example only. Click the picture to make it larger.

4Write and check the note

Check the highlighted Housing enrollment. Make sure it is right for the date of your note.

Fill in Date Contacted, Contact Type, Summary, and the other fields.

Add other residents who were present, if needed. Past notes appear below the form.

CaseNoter suggests wording for common abbreviations and flags possible private information. Review these suggestions as you write.

5Save and check the result

Click Save note to Apricot.

Look for Saved to Apricot and a link to the saved record.

If it says pending, or you are unsure: open Pending requests and check the person's record in Apricot before entering the note again. Ask Dane if you are still unsure.
Write the same note for several residents — click to open

Use this when the same note applies to several residents. Each person gets a separate note in Apricot.

1Choose the site

Click Browse by site. Choose the site from the list on the left.

2Choose the residents

Tick the box beside each person the note applies to.

  • Select all: everyone enrolled at the site.
  • Select all heads of household: one note per family.
  • Clear: untick everyone.

Check the number selected. Click Write note to 11 (the number will match your selection).

Example only. Click the picture to make it larger.

3Write the shared note

Choose Contact Documentation. Meeting Narrative cannot be selected.

Check the list under This note will be saved for:. Each person is matched to their own enrollment at the site.

Fill in the note once. It applies to everyone on the list.

Other Residents Present stays empty here. To link other residents, write a note for one person instead.

4Save and check each person

Click Save 11 notes to Apricot (the number will match your selection).

Check the result for each person. A confirmed save means Apricot received that note.

If any result is pending or unclear, open Pending requests and check Apricot before trying again. Ask Dane if you are unsure.

Click Done when finished.

Need help?

Ask Dane if you get stuck. Do not send your password or Client Secret.

CaseNoter will not install or open — click to open

If Windows blocks it, or Setup cannot finish, ask Dane. If Setup asks you to retry, check your internet connection and try again.

CaseNoter cannot find my old setup — click to open

Leave your old CaseNoter and its black window open, then try Move my existing setup again. If it still cannot find it, ask Dane. Keep your old version in place.

The move did not finish — click to open

Keep your old version in place. Follow any retry message in the new app, or ask Dane. Do not start a new account just to get past the problem.

My new connection details do not work yet — click to open

New Bonterra connection details can take time to become active. Follow the wait or retry instructions in CaseNoter.

I cannot find a participant — click to open

Click Re-sync. Check that you opened the correct team. For Housing, the participant must have a Housing enrollment. If they are still missing, ask Dane.

I forgot my password — click to open

Ask Dane before resetting. Your password cannot be recovered, and resetting removes unfinished work from this computer.

My note has not saved, or I am unsure — click to open

Review Pending requests and check the participant in Apricot before trying again. Ask Dane if the result is still unclear.

The enrollment is wrong — click to open

Choose the correct enrollment from the list before saving.

An update did not finish — click to open

Follow the message in CaseNoter. If it will not reopen or the update keeps failing, ask Dane.

Closing CaseNoter, updates, and changing teams — click to open

Locking and closing

Lock asks for your password again when you return. Finish active saves before closing CaseNoter. Closing its window stops the app. Check any pending or unconfirmed requests before treating your work as saved.

Keep CaseNoter on your taskbar

With CaseNoter open, right-click its icon on the Windows taskbar and choose Pin to taskbar. You can use that icon to open it next time.

Refresh the participant list

If a new participant or enrollment is missing, click Re-sync to refresh the list. This may take a few minutes.

Updates

CaseNoter checks for updates when you open it. Updates install automatically and reopen the app. It may ask you to finish your work before restarting. Follow the message on screen; if you want to keep working, decline and install when you are ready.

Your saved setup and passwords are kept. Updating does not send unfinished work to Apricot. If you are offline, you can keep using the current version.

Already installed the 3.0.1 preview?

Close CaseNoter and run the download on the Get started tab once to upgrade. Choose your folder where CaseNoter is already installed. Your setup and passwords are kept. After this upgrade, future updates happen automatically. Contact Dane if you are unsure which version you have.

Change teams

Close CaseNoter and run the downloaded Setup file again. Choose the other team, finish Setup, and reopen CaseNoter. Each team keeps its own saved setup and password. If you have not connected that team yet, follow the setup prompts or move its existing setup.
