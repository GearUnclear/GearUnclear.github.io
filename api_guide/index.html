Getting Started with CaseNoter

Install the Windows app, bring your setup across, and start writing notes
Using the old browser version? Open the legacy installer guide for CaseNoter 2.6.9.6.

Get CaseNoter on your computer

Download once, choose your team, and open CaseNoter.

Download CaseNoter for Windows

One download includes Housing and Education, Employment & Job Training (EE&T).

Download CaseNoter
Version 3.0.4. Need the old browser version? Open the legacy guide.

1. Run the downloaded file

Open CaseNoter-Setup.exe from your browser's downloads or your Downloads folder. Follow the instructions on screen.

Keep the suggested location unless your computer will not allow it. If that happens, choose a folder under your own Windows account. If Windows blocks the app or you are unsure what to choose, contact Dane for help.

2. Choose your team

Select Housing or Education & Employment (EE&T), then finish Setup. Stay connected to the internet while Setup finishes.

3. Open the new CaseNoter

Setup offers to open CaseNoter. You can also open it from the new desktop shortcut or the Windows Start menu.

Already use CaseNoter? Follow the steps below to bring your existing setup into the new app.

New to CaseNoter? Choose Set up a new account, then follow the Connect to Apricot tab.

Choose the option that fits you. Example with fictional data. Click to enlarge.

Already use CaseNoter?

CaseNoter finds your old setup for you. You do not need to move or copy files.

1. Leave your old CaseNoter open

Open your old CaseNoter the way you normally do. Leave it and its black window running. Finish saving any notes before you begin.

2. Let the new app find your setup

In the new CaseNoter, choose Move my existing setup. It looks for your old setup and opens a request in your browser. If it finds more than one setup, choose the one you normally use.

CaseNoter finds your old setup and asks you to approve the move. Example with fictional data. Click to enlarge.

3. Allow the move

Check that the transfer codes match in the new app and your browser, then click Allow this one-time transfer in your browser.

If the request opens in a different browser account from the one you normally use for CaseNoter, contact Dane for help.

4. Enter your existing password

Return to the new CaseNoter and enter the password you already use for CaseNoter. Click Import and open CaseNoter. The app checks your account, brings your setup across, and opens automatically.

If the app asks to replace an existing setup, continue only if that is the setup you intend to replace. Contact Dane if you are unsure.

5. Check your setup

Your existing passwords stay the same. Check that your team and participant information appear as expected. If you use both teams, check both before removing the old version.

Any unfinished requests brought across appear in Pending requests for review. They are not automatically sent to Apricot. Check Apricot before trying them again.

Cannot find your old setup, or the move does not finish? Leave the old version in place and contact Dane for help.

Connect your own Apricot account

New users only: choose Set up a new account and follow the prompts to choose a password. You will also need two connection details from Bonterra: a Client ID and Client Secret. The steps below show where to get them. If you already use CaseNoter, move your existing setup instead.

Your administrator (Dane) cannot provide the Client ID and Client Secret for you. You must log into the Bonterra account portal yourself to create them.

1 Open the 'Manage APIs' page

Open Bonterra's credential page from the CaseNoter setup screen, or go to account.bonterra.network/api-creds. Log in, open the 'Manage APIs' tab, and click 'Create credentials'.

Daily use

Once setup is done, the steps are the same each day.

1 Unlock

Open CaseNoter from the desktop shortcut or Windows Start menu. Enter your password and click Unlock.

Housing: writing a note for one participant

Use this when you are documenting a single person.

2 Find a participant and choose a note type

Search by name or HMIS number. Only participants with a Housing enrollment appear. Select the participant, then choose Contact Documentation for your case note. Meeting Narrative is being phased out and is disabled.

↓

3 Fill in the note and save

Choose Contact Documentation for your case note. Example with fictional data. Click to enlarge.

CaseNoter matches the Housing enrollment for the note's date and highlights it. Check that it is correct, especially if the participant has more than one enrollment. Fill in the fields (Date Contacted, Contact Type, Summary, and so on), add any other residents present if needed, then click 'Save note to Apricot'. The participant's past notes are listed below the form, so you can review their recent documentation without leaving the page.

Housing: writing notes for a whole site

When the same contact or meeting applies to everyone at a site, you can write the note once and save it to each resident, instead of entering it one person at a time. Each resident still gets their own separate note in Apricot.

1 Switch to 'Browse by site'

At the top of the screen, click the 'Browse by site' tab. Choose a site from the list on the left, such as Fairview PSH. CaseNoter lists everyone currently enrolled there, with a count such as 'Fairview PSH · 23 enrolled'.

↓

2 Choose who the note is for

Check the residents you want to document. Each person shows their HMIS number and enrollment start date. You can also use the buttons above the list:

  • 'Select all' selects everyone enrolled at the site.
  • 'Select all heads of household' selects only heads of household, which is useful when you want one note per family.
  • 'Clear' removes the current selection.

A bar at the bottom shows how many you have selected, such as '11 participants selected'. Click 'Write note to 11' to continue.

↓

3 Choose the note type

Choose Contact Documentation for the selected residents. Meeting Narrative is being phased out and is disabled.

↓

4 Write the note once

Under 'This note will be saved for:' you will see the full list of residents, each with their housing program and enrollment date. CaseNoter has already matched each resident to their own enrollment at the site, so you do not choose enrollments one by one. Fill in the fields once, and they apply to everyone on the list.

'Other Residents Present' is left empty in batch mode. If you need to link other residents to a note, use the single-participant flow.
↓

5 Save the notes

Click 'Save 11 notes to Apricot'. CaseNoter creates a separate note for each resident, against that resident's own enrollment, and shows a result for each one.

Check the result for each resident. Only a confirmed save to Apricot means the note was received. If a result is pending or unclear, review it in Pending requests and check Apricot before trying again. Contact Dane if you are unsure. Click 'Done' when you are finished.

Check that your note saved

Look for Saved to Apricot and the link to the saved record. A pending or unconfirmed request does not mean Apricot received it. If the connection drops or the result is unclear, check the participant's record in Apricot before entering the note again.

Using CaseNoter day to day

Open it from your desktop or Start menu whenever you need it.

Locking and closing

Lock asks for your password again when you return. Finish active saves before closing CaseNoter. Closing its window stops the app. Check any pending or unconfirmed requests before treating your work as saved.

Keep CaseNoter on your taskbar

With CaseNoter open, right-click its icon on the Windows taskbar and choose Pin to taskbar. You can use that icon to open it next time.

Refresh the participant list

If a new participant or enrollment is missing, click Re-sync to refresh the list. This may take a few minutes.

Updates

CaseNoter checks for updates when you open it. Updates install automatically and reopen the app. It may ask you to finish your work before restarting. Follow the message on screen; if you want to keep working, decline and install when you are ready.

Your saved setup and passwords are kept. Updating does not send unfinished requests to Apricot. If you are offline, you can keep using the current version.

Already installed the 3.0.1 preview?

Close CaseNoter and run the download on the Get started tab once to upgrade. Choose your existing installation location. Your setup and passwords are kept. After this upgrade, future updates happen automatically. Contact Dane if you are unsure which version you have.

Change teams

Close CaseNoter and run the downloaded Setup file again. Choose the other team, finish Setup, and reopen CaseNoter. Each team keeps its own saved setup and password. If you have not connected that team yet, follow the setup prompts or move its existing setup.
